Three ways a SIP ends

The industry counts all of these as “stopped”, but each needs a different fix.

1. The investor chooses to stop

88%of at-risk stories describe a choice: stopping, pausing or thinking about it
“Covid 2021 me pura damage ho gya market isliye mai band kr diya”YouTube

2. It breaks on its own

12%of at-risk stories involve a failed payment or a fund closing to new SIPs

3. The investor switches

1 in 6stopped SIPs moved to another fund or app; the investor kept investing
