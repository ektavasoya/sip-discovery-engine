Not every ending is the same

Chosen: the investor decides to stop, pause or reduce.

Not chosen: 12% of at-risk stories involve a failed payment or a fund closing to new SIPs.

Switches: 1 in 6 stopped SIPs moved to another fund or app; the investor kept investing.
